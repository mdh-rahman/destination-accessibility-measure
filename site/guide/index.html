Introduction

Destination Accessibility Measure

DAM is a reproducible workflow for measuring how people reach jobs and essential destinations by different travel modes and at different times of day.

The central rule: create or ingest a travel-time matrix first, then calculate a clearly named accessibility measure. Routing and measurement are documented separately.

What DAM provides

  • Mode-specific travel-time inputs with explicit dates and assumptions
  • Reviewed and documented destination inventories
  • Consistent measures calculated with the R accessibility package
  • Coverage and plausibility checks that retain unreachable origins
  • Tidy, GIS-ready outputs and run manifests

What DAM measures

DAM supports questions such as how many jobs are reachable within 45 minutes, how long it takes to reach the third-nearest grocery, and how much eligible park acreage is reachable within 30 minutes.

First case study

The Austin-Round Rock-Georgetown implementation covers Bastrop, Caldwell, Hays, Travis, and Williamson counties. Austin is an example configuration rather than a limit on the framework.
