Methods

Accessibility measures

Measure names state the question directly. The appropriate measure depends on how a destination functions and the amount of choice or supply that matters.

MeasureQuestionAustin examples
Reachable opportunitiesHow many opportunities are reachable within a stated time?Jobs; park count and acreage
Travel time to kth nearestHow long does it take to reach a practical amount of choice?Third grocery; fifth childcare; nearest library or pharmacy
Gravity accessHow much opportunity remains after continuous travel-time decay?Optional sensitivity measure
Supply-sensitive accessHow much capacity is available relative to reachable demand?Potential healthcare or childcare extension

Austin measures currently declared

  • Jobs reachable within 15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes
  • Travel time to the third-nearest grocery
  • Travel time to the fifth-nearest childcare facility
  • Park count and acreage reachable within 10, 15, and 30 minutes
  • Nearest library, pharmacy, and public center
  • Second-nearest hospital and third-nearest urgent care
