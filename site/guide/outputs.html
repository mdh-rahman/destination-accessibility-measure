Outputs and quality checks

Every run produces a result, a compact QA summary, and a machine-readable record of how it was created.

Output contract

<scenario>/<mode>/<period>/
├── accessibility.csv
├── qa_summary.csv
└── run_manifest.json

Required checks

  • Origin and destination geographic coverage
  • Missing or duplicate identifiers and coordinates
  • Destination eligibility and exclusions
  • GTFS service-calendar validity for transit runs
  • Travel-time distributions and implausible values
  • Unreachable and missing origin counts
  • Matrix, inventory, configuration, and package provenance

Presentation layers

Web maps, GeoPackages, hosted layers, and future ArcGIS packages are derived from validated analytical outputs. Restricted source records and routing caches remain outside public delivery packages.
