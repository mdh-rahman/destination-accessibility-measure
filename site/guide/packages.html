Packages and inputs

DAM records the role of each package so routing software is not confused with the accessibility calculator.

Package or inputRole in Austin
accessibilityCalculates cumulative, kth-nearest, gravity, and supply-sensitive indicators
r5rGenerates transit plus walk travel-time matrices
GTFSSupplies transit routes, stops, schedules, and service calendars
OpenStreetMapSupplies the pedestrian and street network used by r5r
ArcGIS H8 matricesSupply automobile travel times for peak and midday periods
sf and h3jsrSpatial processing and H3 conversion
dplyr and readrTabular transformations and input-output
yaml and jsonliteScenario configuration and run manifests

GTFS, OpenStreetMap, and r5r are required to reproduce Austin transit routing. They are not required merely to calculate a measure from an existing, validated matrix.
