Austin case study

Routing and measurement

Austin uses different travel-time sources for automobile and transit plus walk, followed by one common accessibility calculator.

Automobile

Time-of-day H3 resolution 8 travel-time matrices prepared by the Austin Opportunity Index project team in ArcGIS are the current authoritative automobile input. DAM subsets and standardizes those prepared matrices; it does not recreate the ArcGIS network analysis.

Transit plus walk

r5r generates travel-time matrices from a pinned CapMetro GTFS feed and OpenStreetMap network. The calculation includes walking access and egress, waiting, transfers, and in-vehicle time. Current analyses use AM peak and midday departure windows and allow trips up to 90 minutes.

Common measurement

The R accessibility package calculates destination and job indicators from either matrix. It is the measurement engine, not a routing engine.
