How to interpret this

Accessibility is the same for everyone starting in the same H3 cell. Selecting a group keeps accessibility as the map color and emphasizes cells where that group has a larger local share. The summary is weighted by the estimated residents or households in that group.

ACS 2024 five-year counts are allocated using 2020 block population. Household allocations also use population as a proxy. Disability is available at tract scale; other groups use block groups. Small-area estimates and within-area locations are uncertain. Austin summaries use cells whose centers fall inside the full-purpose boundary.

The four race/ethnicity categories are mutually exclusive. “Other non-Hispanic” combines Asian, American Indian/Alaska Native, Pacific Islander, other-race and multiracial residents. The optional people-of-color summary overlaps these categories.

Transit uses the pinned CapMetro network and does not represent every regional provider. Park routing uses representative polygon points rather than verified entrances; acreage includes the entire reachable park.
