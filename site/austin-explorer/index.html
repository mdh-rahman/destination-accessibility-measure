How to interpret this

Group outcomes weight each H3 origin’s accessibility by estimated residents or households. They describe average conditions, not individual trips or needs. No destination-importance weights are applied.

ACS 2024 five-year counts are allocated using 2020 block population. Household allocations also use population as a proxy. Disability is available at tract scale; other maps use block groups. Small-area estimates and within-area locations are uncertain. Austin summaries use cells whose centers fall inside the full-purpose boundary.

The four race/ethnicity categories are mutually exclusive. “Other non-Hispanic” combines Asian, American Indian/Alaska Native, Pacific Islander, other-race and multiracial residents. The optional people-of-color summary overlaps these categories and must not be added to them.

Transit uses the pinned CapMetro network; results do not represent all regional transit providers. Park travel times use representative polygon points rather than verified entrances; acreage includes the entire reachable park.
