Destination Accessibility Measure

A reproducible framework for measuring access to jobs and essential destinations using mode-appropriate travel times, reviewed opportunity data, transparent measures, and GIS-ready outputs.

DAM separates routing from measurement. Travel-time matrices may come from different engines; the R accessibility package applies consistent destination measures to each.

Frame

Define origins, geography, modes, periods, and population context.

Prepare travel times

Build or ingest mode-specific origin-destination matrices.

Measure and validate

Calculate named indicators, retain unreachable origins, and publish QA evidence.
